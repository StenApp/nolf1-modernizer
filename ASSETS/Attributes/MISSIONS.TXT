/************************************************************************
**
** M I S S I O N   R A T I N G
**
** The MissionRating definition specifies the global values for calculating
** the mission and player rating values.
**
** The mission rating definition should contain the following properties:
**
**	SuccessPoints (Integer)
**
**		The number of points rewarded for successfully completing a mission.
**
**	IntelPoints (Integer)
**
**		The number of points rewarded for obtaining all of the intelligence
**		material in a mission.
**
**	ReputationInc (Integer)
**
**		How much the player's reputation is increased for each reputation point
**		recieved.
**
**	ReputationMax (Integer)
**
**		The highest reputation the player may achieve.
**
**	HealthInc (Float)
**
**		The percentage the player's max health capacity is increased for 
**		each health point recieved (a value between 0 and 1.0).
**
**	HealthMax (Float)
**
**		The maximum percentage the player's health can be increased (1.0 = 100%
**		the player's default health value)
**
**	ArmorInc (Float)
**
**		The percentage the player's max Armor capacity is increased for 
**		each Armor point recieved (a value between 0 and 1.0).
**
**	ArmorMax (Float)
**
**		The maximum percentage the player's Armor can be increased (1.0 = 100%
**		the player's default Armor value)
**
**	AmmoInc (Float)
**
**		The percentage the player's max Ammo capacity is increased for 
**		each Ammo point recieved (a value between 0 and 1.0).
**
**	AmmoMax (Float)
**
**		The maximum percentage the player's Ammo can be increased (1.0 = 100%
**		the player's default Ammo value)
**
**	DamageInc (Float)
**
**		The percentage the damage player inflicts is increased for 
**		each Damage point recieved (a value between 0 and 1.0).
**
**	DamageMax (Float)
**
**		The maximum percentage the player's damage can be increased (1.0 = 100%
**		the player's default Damage value)
**
**	PerturbDec (Float)
**
**		The percentage the perturb for the player's shots is decreased for 
**		each Perturb point recieved (a value between 0 and 1.0).
**
**	PerturbMin (Float)
**
**		The minimum percentage to which the player's perturb can be decreased.
**
**	StealthDec (Float)
**
**		The percentage the sound of the player's footsteps is decreased for 
**		each Stealth point recieved (a value between 0 and 1.0).
**
**	StealthMin (Float)
**
**		The minimum percentage to which the player's noise can be decreased.
**
**
*/

/************************************************************************
**
** A W A R D S
**
** The Awards definition specifies the global values for calculating
** player awards.
**
** The awards definition should contain the following properties:
**
**	Accuracy (Float)
**
**		The minimum accuracy percentage needed to earn an accuracy award.
**
**	Marksman (Float)
**
**		The minimum head-shot percentage needed to earn a marksmanship award.
**
**	HighAmmoAward (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Ids (stored in CRes.dll) of awards given
**		for high ammo use. (No more than 10 are allowed)
**
**	LowAmmoAward (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Ids (stored in CRes.dll) of awards given
**		for low ammo use. (No more than 10 are allowed)
**
**	MarksmanAward (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Ids (stored in CRes.dll) of awards given
**		for high percentage of head shots. (No more than 10 are allowed)
**
**	AccuracyAward (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Ids (stored in CRes.dll) of awards given
**		for high percentage of hits. (No more than 10 are allowed)
**
**	NonInjuryAward (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Ids (stored in CRes.dll) of awards given
**		for avoiding health loss. (No more than 10 are allowed)
**
**	NotShotAward (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Ids (stored in CRes.dll) of awards given
**		for avoiding being shot. (No more than 10 are allowed)
**
**	StealthAward (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Ids (stored in CRes.dll) of awards given
**		for avoiding detection. (No more than 10 are allowed)
**
**/

/************************************************************************
**
** M I S S I O N
**
** Mission definitions specify what levels are in the game.  The 
** ordering of the missions are the same as they appear in this file
** (Mission0, Mission1, Mission2, etc.)
**
** All mission definitions should contain the following properties:
**
**	NameId (Integer)
**
**		The Id (stored in CRes.dll)	of the text string containing the
**		name of the mission.
**
**	DescriptionId (Integer)
**
**		The Id (stored in CRes.dll)	of the text containing a brief 
**		description of the mission.
**
**	BriefingId (Integer)
**
**		The Id (stored in CRes.dll)	of the text containing a detailed
**		explanaation the mission.
**
**	ObjectiveIds (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Ids (stored in CRes.dll) of objectives
**		known at the start of the mission.
**
**	Photo (String)
**
**		The file name for photo associated with the mission
**		(e.g., interface\0000club.pcx).
**
**	DefaultWeapons (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of weapon names that should be chosen by default
**		for the mission. These weapons will be selected on the outfitting screen
**		and also added to the list of available weapons.
**		(e.g., "P38,AK47" ).
**
**	AllowedWeapons (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of weapon names that should be added to the list
**		of weapons available for the mission.
**		Note: Once a weapon is allowed, it remains available in future 
**		missions and doesn't need to be listed again.
**
**	RequiredWeapons (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of weapon names that are mandatory for the
**		mission. The player will not be able to unselect this item in
**		the outfitting screen. 
**		Note: This weapon will be considered available in future missions.
**
**	OneTimeWeapons (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of weapon names that are mandatory for the
**		mission, but are not automatically available in future levels.
**
**	DeniedWeapons (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of weapon names that are not allowed for this
**		mission. If they were available in prior missions, they will be
**		available in future missions.  
**
**	DefaultGadgets (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of gadget names that should be chosen by default
**		for the mission. These gadgets will be selected on the outfitting screen
**		and also added to the list of available gadgets.
**		(e.g., "Camera Disabler,Code Breaker" ).
**
**	AllowedGadgets (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of gadget names that should be added to the list
**		of gadgets available for the mission.
**		Note: Once a gadget is allowed, it remains available in future 
**		missions and doesn't need to be listed again.
**
**	RequiredGadgets (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of gadget names that are mandatory for the
**		mission. The player will not be able to unselect this item in
**		the outfitting screen. 
**		Note: This gadget will be considered available in future missions.
**
**	OneTimeGadgets (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of gadget names that are mandatory for the
**		mission, but are not automatically available in future levels.
**
**	DeniedGadgets (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of gadget names that are not allowed for this
**		mission. If they were available in prior missions, they will be
**		available in future missions.  
**
**	AllowedAmmo (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of ammo names that should be added to the list
**		of ammos types available for the mission.
**		Note: Once a ammo type is allowed, it remains available in future 
**		missions and doesn't need to be listed again.
**
**	RequiredAmmo (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of ammo names that are mandatory for the
**		mission, and are automatically available in future missions.
**
**	OneTimeAmmo (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of ammo names that are mandatory for the
**		mission, but are not automatically available in future missions.
**
**	DeniedAmmo (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of ammo names that are not allowed for this
**		mission. If they were available in prior missions, they will be
**		available in future missions. 
**
**	AllowedMods (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of mod names that should be added to the list
**		of mods available for the mission.
**		Note: Once a mod is allowed, it remains available in future 
**		missions and doesn't need to be listed again.
**
**	RequiredMods (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of mod names that are mandatory for the
**		mission. The player will not be able to unselect this item in
**		the outfitting screen. 
**		Note: This mod will be considered available in future missions.
**
**	AllowedGear (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Gear names that should be added to the list
**		of Gears types available for the mission.
**		Note: Once a Gear type is allowed, it remains available in future 
**		missions and doesn't need to be listed again.
**
**	RequiredGear (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Gear names that are mandatory for the
**		mission, and are automatically available in future missions.
**
**	OneTimeGear (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Gear names that are mandatory for the
**		mission, but are not automatically available in future missions.
**
**	DeniedGear (String)
**
**		A comma separated list of Gear names that are not allowed for this
**		mission. If they were available in prior missions, they will be
**		available in future missions. 
**
**	NumWeapons (Integer)
**
**		Number of total weapons that can be selected for this mission 
**		(-1 indicates no limit).
**
**	NumGadgets (Integer)
**
**		Number of total gadgets that can be selected for this mission 
**		(-1 indicates no limit).
**
**	NumAmmo (Integer)
**
**		Number of total ammo types that can be selected for this mission 
**		(-1 indicates no limit).
**
**	NumSupplies (Integer)
**
**		Number of total gadget supplies types that can be selected for this mission 
**		(-1 indicates no limit).
**
**	NumWeaponMods (Integer)
**
**		Number of weapon mods that can be selected for this mission 
**		(-1 indicates no limit).
**
**	NumGear (Integer)
**
**		Number of total gear types that can be selected for this mission 
**		(-1 indicates no limit).
**
**	AllowAwards (Integer)
**
**		Determines whether performance awards will be given for this mission.
**		(0 indictes no awards will be given)
**
**	HighAmmoUsage (Integer)
**
**		Mininum number of shots needed to get the high ammo use award.
**		(-1 indicates award is not available).
**
**	LowAmmoUsage (Integer)
**
**		Maxinum number of shots allowed to get the low ammo use award.
**		(-1 indicates award is not available).
**
**	MaximumDetections (Integer)
**
**		Maxinum number of detections and bodies found allowed to get the stealth award.
**		(-1 indicates award is not available).
**
**	Rank??? (String)
**
**		The list of bonuses given for achieving the rank for this mission. 
**		These are cumulative bonuses, so achieving Rank6 gives all of the 
**		bonuses from Rank0 through Rank6. The bonuses are in a comma separated
**		list with each item in the list consisting of a category and the number
**		of points earned in that category. The category names may be abbreviated
**		to as little as the their first letter. (or first 2 letters for Ammo and Armor)
**		The categories are:
**			Reputation, Health, Armor, Ammo, Perturb, Damage, Stealth
**
**		Example:
**			Rank6 = "H 1, Ar 1, Rep 1, Am 1"
**			(gives a bonus of 1 point each to Health, Armor, Reputation, and Ammo)
**	
**	
**	Level0-??? (String)
**
**		The file name for each level (scene) file (e.g., Worlds\m01s01).
**		associated with this mission.  Multiple levels may be specified
**		(e.g., Level0 = "xxx", Level1 = "yyy", etc.).
**	
**		NOTE:  the .dat extension should NOT be included.
*/
